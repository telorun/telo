## 0.12.1 - 2026-08-15
### Fixed
* 'amount' and 'reserved' are declared `type: integer`, so they reach the controller as an int64 whenever CEL or another resource's output produced them — and `Number.isInteger` is false for one while `amount - reserved` throws. A settle fed by the matching reserve's own result was rejected as a non-integer. Both are read through `integerInput`, which accepts either representation.
