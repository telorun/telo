Runners gain a durable session store and renamed session settings. `RUNNER_SESSION_STORE` selects `sqlite` (default, Node's built-in SQLite in `sessions.sqlite` under `RUNNER_STATE_DIR`) or `postgres` (`RUNNER_SESSION_STORE_URL`); it keeps each session's owner, workspace name, agent, app set, config, status and times, never env or tokens, with one session per owner and workspace, plus the DPoP `jti`s seen, under a versioned schema whose migrations carry records forward. The k8s and docker runners open it at boot and refuse to start with SQLite and no `RUNNER_STATE_DIR`. Session settings are renamed: `RUNNER_WATCH_MAX_SESSIONS` → `RUNNER_MAX_SESSIONS` (default 8, running sessions), `RUNNER_WATCH_IDLE_SECONDS` → `RUNNER_SESSION_IDLE_SECONDS`, `RUNNER_WATCH_MAX_TTL_SECONDS` → `RUNNER_SESSION_MAX_RUNNING_SECONDS`, `RUNNER_WATCH_SUSPENDED_TTL_SECONDS` → `RUNNER_SESSION_RETENTION_SECONDS` (default 30 days), `RUNNER_WATCH_RELOAD_LIMIT` → `RUNNER_SESSION_RELOAD_LIMIT`; `RUNNER_WATCH_SESSIONS` (every session is now a workspace session, so there is nothing to switch on), `RUNNER_WORKSPACE_CHECKPOINT_SECONDS`, `RUNNER_EXIT_TTL_MS`, `RUNNER_MAX_TTL_SECONDS` and `RUNNER_APP_MAX_TTL_SECONDS` are removed, and a runner started with any of them set refuses to boot, naming the replacement. The k8s runner reads `RUNNER_SESSION_STORAGE_SIZE` (default `5Gi`) and `RUNNER_SESSION_STORAGE_CLASS`, and its chart's `watch.*` values become `sessions.idleSeconds` / `maxRunningSeconds` / `retentionSeconds` / `reloadLimit`, with `runner.maxSessions` defaulting to 8 and `limits.maxTtlSeconds` gone.
