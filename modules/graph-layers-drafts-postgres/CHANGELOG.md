# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-10
### Added
* New PostgreSQL module: the drafts strategy for layered knowledge graphs. NodeTable and RelationshipTable extend the engine's Table, taking the author's columns and adding the draft bookkeeping as internal columns with their indexes; LayersTable and DraftsTable are the two bookkeeping tables, of which the author names only the physical table; Node, Relationship and Store declare the types and one layer each, optionally built on an ordered list of base layers. A layer keeps one published row and at most one draft row per key, one open draft at a time - enforced by the database - and only its current revision. Changes are staged in a draft and published together, conditionally on the revision the draft stands on; conflicts are compared row against row, so two sides changing different properties of one key collide. A write that changes nothing the layer states advances no revision, and a read never writes. A table kind is held to its engine table's rules, restated on its own fields under the engine's codes, and reserves the graph_ prefix for column and index names in its schema. A merge or create of a key the store's view does not show - never stated, or hidden by the layer's own removal - stores the given properties plus the declared default of every other one.
