The k8s runner serves the durable workspace session (breaking). Each session gets a PersistentVolumeClaim of its own, `telo-session-<id>` (`RUNNER_SESSION_STORAGE_SIZE`, `RUNNER_SESSION_STORAGE_CLASS`; chart `sessionStorage.size` / `.storageClass`), owned by no pod: `workspace/` is mounted at `/workspace` (its `.telo` module cache included) and `agent/` at `/var/lib/telo-agent-state` for the co-resident agent, whose `TELO_HOST_STATE_DIR` points there. Suspend deletes the pod and keeps the claim, resume and app-set or agent changes mount it again, and deleting the session or retention deletes both. Every `app-<name>` container is idle until Run, running the session supervisor at `/opt/telo-runner/supervisor` (copied out of the runner's own image by a `supervisor` init container), with no TTY, no stdin and no session env — only `TELO_SUPERVISOR_LISTEN` (`0.0.0.0:<9330 + position>`) and a per-container `TELO_SUPERVISOR_TOKEN`; each app's supervisor port and debug port (`9230 + position`) are reserved, and an app declaring one is refused `400 port_conflict` (or `rejected` on reload). A container that ends, or a supervisor that restarted, fails the session naming the container. At boot the runner adopts each running session's pod — reading every supervisor token and the agent's token back from the pod spec, reconnecting, and reporting live runs as `running` — suspends a session whose pod is gone, stale or of another supervisor protocol generation (resume rebuilds it), and its orphan sweep spares every known session's claim. New required `RUNNER_SUPERVISOR_IMAGE` (the chart sets it to the runner's image); `RUNNER_SELF_URL`, `RUNNER_INIT_IMAGE` and the chart's `session.initImage` are gone and refused, with one-shot run pods, catalog app pods, the bundle fetch and the `pods/attach` permission. `RUNNER_MAX_CPU` / `_MEMORY` / `_EPHEMERAL_STORAGE` size each app container and the `workspace` container, `RUNNER_APP_MAX_*` the agent's (chart `limits.agent.*`). The chart adds the runner's own claim `telo-k8s-runner-state` at `RUNNER_STATE_DIR` for the SQLite store, `sessionStore.kind` / `.url` / `.existingSecret` for Postgres, `identity.audience` / `.issuers`, and PersistentVolumeClaim RBAC. The image gains a static musl session supervisor, cross-built per architecture.

App supervisors are driven through runner-core's `SupervisedApp`, and the test cluster's supervisors are runner-core's `FakeSupervisor`.
