## 0.8.0 - 2026-08-11
### Added
* Schedule.Interval and Schedule.Cron log a firing tick, a tick skipped by a closed 'when' gate, and each re-arm at debug. A skipped tick is otherwise indistinguishable from one that never came due — the schedule simply goes quiet. A failing tick now reports the thrown value as the record's structured error, keeping the type, stack and cause chain that the previous message-only attribute discarded.
