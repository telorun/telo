"@telorun/analyzer": minor
---

`telo check` now checks every member of an `x-telo-scope` field (a `Run.Sequence`'s `with:`, or any kind's scope) as a resource of its own: its fields against its kind, its references, its CEL, contracts, value types, rules and deprecations, each finding reported at the member's place inside the resource declaring the scope (`with[0].port`). Before, a misspelled field, an unresolved `!ref` or a CEL typo in a member passed the check and failed only when the scope opened. Names resolve as the kernel resolves them — the scope's members first, innermost scope out, then the module — so a member may reuse a module-level name, which it shadows inside its scope; two members of one scope sharing a name are `DUPLICATE_RESOURCE_NAME`; members holding each other are `DEPENDENCY_CYCLE`; `resources.<member>` is readable only inside the regions of the resource declaring the scope, outside fields evaluated at load — not in a member's own fields and not inside a nested scope, where a sibling is reached with `!ref`; such a read is one `CEL_UNKNOWN_FIELD` naming the scope that declares the member — and, in an inline declaration written beside a `with:` block (a step's `invoke: { kind: … }`), one `CEL_UNKNOWN_FIELD` saying the declaration is created outside that scope and how to reach the member, where it was also `SCOPED_NAME_OUT_OF_REACH` before; `SCOPED_NAME_OUT_OF_REACH` now reports `!ref` references alone, and `OBSERVED_STATE_IN_STARTUP_FIELD` no longer reports a provider's status read from a scoped sibling's field; a sink declared in a scope is `SINK_UNATTACHED`. In the module graph a member is one box of its holder's scope, whose region names the scope field (`with`) rather than each region it is visible in.
