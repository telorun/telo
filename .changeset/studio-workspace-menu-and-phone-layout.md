Studio switches and closes workspaces from one menu in the top bar, and is usable at phone width.

The workspace menu lists what the device can open without asking — the browser-stored workspace and the Telo Cloud working copies it holds, signed in or not — beside "Open folder…", "Open from Telo Cloud…" and "Close workspace". A closed workspace stays closed across a reload. The "No workspace open" screen names the browser workspace ("Open browser workspace") and offers Telo Cloud beside it. The top bar drops the permanently disabled "Save", shows Settings and the Cloud account as icons, and carries the workspace, module and telo version on its left.

Below 768px the sidebar is a drawer, the top bar folds Undo, Redo and the theme into one menu, the agent panel and the topology's detail panel cover the editor, and the topology's two side columns open over the canvas instead of beside it. Every control that appeared only on hover — a tab's close button among them — is shown on a touch screen.
